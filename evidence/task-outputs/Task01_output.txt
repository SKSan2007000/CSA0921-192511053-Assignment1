CAMPUS SMART PARKING ? PROBLEM ANALYSIS
INPUT                    | PROCESS                                | OUTPUT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Registration plate       | Normalize and check duplicates         | Valid vehicle identity
Vehicle/user category    | Select eligible slot and rate          | Assigned bay and hourly rate
Entry/exit time          | Calculate elapsed and billed hours     | Parking fee
EV kWh                   | Multiply by charging tariff            | Separate charging fee

Core objects: Vehicle, ParkingSlot, ParkingTicket, ParkingFacility.
Constraints: one vehicle per slot; no duplicate active plate; reserved bays are protected.
